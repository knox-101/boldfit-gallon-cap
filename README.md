# Boldfit 2.2 L Gallon bottle – replacement cap system

Bottle: **Boldfit 2.2 L Gym Gallon, model `GallonArmygreen2.2New`** (PETG, wide mouth,
screw-on flip-top lid with a silicone gasket, made in China –
[Amazon.in B0CNRSGV7Q](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0CNRSGV7Q)).
No manufacturer drawing or thread spec exists publicly, so this project is **parametric**:
measure the neck once, type the numbers into `scad/params.scad`, re-render, print.

```
boldfit-gallon-cap/
├─ README.md            <- you are here
├─ scad/                <- OpenSCAD sources
│   ├─ params.scad      <- NECK MEASUREMENTS + fit tuning (the only file you must edit)
│   ├─ threads.scad     <- helical thread generator + neck model
│   ├─ cap_lib.scad     <- shared cap body (grip, gasket seat, female thread, D-loop)
│   ├─ parts.scad       <- every printable part as a module
│   ├─ 01…05_*.scad     <- one file per STL: open, F6, export
│   └─ view_section_*   <- F5-only cut-away assemblies for sanity checks
├─ stl/                 <- rendered STLs (currently from PLACEHOLDER neck numbers!)
├─ preview/             <- PNG previews, cut-away sections, overview sheet
├─ tools/               <- portable OpenSCAD nightly (fast Manifold renders)
└─ render_all.cmd       <- regenerates every STL + preview in ~10 s
```

> **Status:** the neck dimensions in `params.scad` are educated guesses for this family of
> jug (≈ 60 mm thread, 50 mm mouth, 2-start coarse thread). **Do not print a full cap
> until you have replaced them with caliper readings** – print `01_test_ring` first.

## 1. The four designs

| # | Name | What it is | Parts | Why you would pick it |
|---|------|-----------|-------|-----------------------|
| 01 | **Test ring** | Threaded skirt only, no top | 1 | Prints in ~12 min. Proves the thread fit before you spend 2 h on a cap. |
| 02 | **Vault** | Plain storage cap, scalloped grip, D-loop for the Boldfit strap | 1 + neck gasket | Simplest, most leak-proof. One moving part: none. |
| 03 | **Flip** | Flip-top drinking lid: 22 mm mouth, filament-pin hinge, snap latch, removable TPU plug seal with a coloured "button" showing on top | 3 + neck gasket + 26 mm of filament | Closest to the original. One-handed drinking. |
| 04 | **Port** | Big cap with a 30 mm threaded drinking port in the middle, closed by a quarter-turn screw plug with its own flat gasket | 3 + neck gasket | Zero hinges or snaps to wear out, every surface unscrews for washing, very hard to leak. |

All caps share the same **body**: outer top face printed on the bed, skirt up, no supports,
female thread generated by subtracting the *measured neck + clearance*. Fix the thread once
in `params.scad` and all four designs are correct.

### Hygiene rules baked into every part
* **No blind pockets.** The thread groove runs out of the skirt mouth, the hinge pin hole is
  a through-hole, the port's female thread is a through-tube. Water flushes everything.
* **Every soft seal lifts out.** Neck gasket sits in a 0.8 mm open seat with a chamfered
  edge (clicks in, peels out with a fingernail). Flip plug seal pulls out by its button.
  Port gasket is a loose flat ring.
* **Every inside corner is chamfered**, grip is wide scallops (wipe, do not scrub), no ribs,
  no knurl, no text recesses by default.
* Print the caps in **PETG** (same material as the bottle, dishwasher-safe-ish, no
  moisture-fed cracking like PLA) with **100 % infill or ≥ 6 walls** so there are no hollow
  cavities for water to soak into. Sand nothing on the inside; leave the smooth bed face as
  the outside.

### Waterproofing – two seals
* **Neck:** 2 mm TPU/silicone flat gasket, 1.2 mm proud of its seat, crushed between the
  bottle lip and the cap ceiling as you tighten. Thread engagement ≥ 1.25 turns per start.
* **Mouth (Flip):** tapered TPU plug 0.5 mm oversize squeezing into a plain 22 mm hole with
  a 5 mm straight land, plus the plug's flange touching the cap top as a secondary face seal.
  Latch holds it with a 0.7 mm snap.
* **Mouth (Port):** 1.5 mm flat TPU gasket under the plug head compressed by a 2-start
  thread (quarter turn = full compression). Same principle as the neck, just smaller.

## 2. How to measure the neck (10 numbers, 5 minutes)

You need digital calipers. A steel rule works for pitch; it does not for diameters.
Write the numbers straight into `scad/params.scad`.

| Param | How to measure |
|-------|----------------|
| `neck_major_d` | Jaws across the neck **over the thread crests**, at the widest. Rotate 90° and average. |
| `neck_minor_d` | Jaws across the bare neck wall **between** thread turns (the root). If the thread is too tight to get in, measure the plain band just under the lip. |
| `mouth_id` | Inside jaws across the opening. |
| `neck_height` | Depth gauge from the top lip down to the shoulder / collar ring where the cap used to stop. |
| `thread_pitch` | Distance between two neighbouring crests **measured straight down the axis** (not along the slant). |
| `thread_starts` | Look straight at the lip: how many separate thread ridges begin near the top edge? 1 or 2 (occasionally 3). Two starts spaced 180° apart is the common case on these jugs. |
| `thread_turns` | Follow ONE ridge round the neck: how many full laps? Typical 1 – 1.5. |
| `thread_start_z` | Lip down to the top edge of the first thread ridge. |
| `thread_crest_w` | Axial width of the flat top of a ridge. |
| `thread_root_w` | Axial width of a ridge where it meets the neck. If the ridge is a semicircle, root ≈ 2 × crest. |

Pitch sanity check: `thread_pitch × thread_starts` must equal how far one ridge climbs
in one full lap (the **lead**). If a ridge climbs 12 mm per lap and ridges are 6 mm apart,
you have 2 starts.

Also tell me (not parameters, but they change the design):
* Does the **Boldfit carry strap** clip to a ring that sits *under* the cap on the neck, or
  did it hang off the cap itself? (Photos suggest a neck ring. If the ring is also gone, I
  can add a collar.)
* Is the original **silicone gasket** gone too? (Assumed yes.)
* **Printer / nozzle / materials** you have: PETG? TPU? If no TPU, the neck gasket can be cut
  from a 2 mm food-grade silicone sheet with the gasket STL as a template.

## 3. Iteration loop

1. Enter measurements in `scad/params.scad`.
2. Render `01_test_ring.scad` → print (PETG, 0.2 mm, 4 walls).
3. Screw it on:
   * Will not start / very tight → `radial_clear += 0.10`
   * Wobbles / strips easily → `radial_clear -= 0.10`
   * Starts, then binds half-way → pitch or starts wrong; re-measure the lead
   * Bottoms out before the lip → `thread_start_z` or `thread_turns` wrong
4. Re-render the ring until it spins on smoothly with a little drag at the end.
5. Render + print the cap you want and the matching gaskets.

### Rendering
Two OpenSCAD builds are available:

* **Fast (recommended):** portable nightly in `tools/OpenSCAD-nightly/` – renders every part in
  under a second with the Manifold backend. Double-click `render_all.cmd` to regenerate all STLs
  after editing `params.scad`, or run one part:

```bash
tools/OpenSCAD-nightly/openscad.com --backend=Manifold -o stl/01_test_ring.stl scad/01_test_ring.scad
```

* **Installed:** OpenSCAD 2021.01 at `C:\Program Files\OpenSCAD` (Start menu). Fine for opening
  files and F5 previews; F6 renders take 1–8 minutes each.

Open any `.scad` in either build, press **F5** to preview, **F6** to render, **F7** to export.

## 4. Print settings

| Part | Material | Orientation (as modelled) | Layer | Walls / infill | Notes |
|------|----------|---------------------------|-------|----------------|-------|
| Test ring | PETG | as is | 0.2 | 4 walls | 12 min |
| Vault / Flip base / Port cap | PETG | top face on bed, skirt up | 0.16–0.2 | 6 walls, 100 % | ~2 h. No supports. Slow outer wall for clean thread. |
| Flip lid | PETG | outer face down | 0.16 | 5 walls, 100 % | no supports |
| Port plug | PETG | head down | 0.16 | 5 walls, 100 % | no supports |
| Neck gasket, port gasket | TPU 95A | flat | 0.2 | 100 %, 20 mm/s | or cut from 2 mm silicone sheet |
| Flip plug seal | TPU 95A | button down | 0.12–0.16 | 100 %, 20 mm/s | retraction off / minimal |

Flip hinge pin: 26 mm of 1.75 mm filament pushed through both ears and the knuckle; touch
each end with a soldering iron or lighter to mushroom it. Cut it off to disassemble.

## 6. Design notes / known assumptions
* Right-hand threads throughout (standard bottle direction).
* Thread form is trapezoidal with flat crest – prints cleanly, tolerates FDM rounding.
* Female thread = male geometry grown by `radial_clear` and `axial_clear`, so male and
  female always match by construction.
* The Flip lid opens ~115° and stops on the hinge ears.
* Nothing here is food-contact *certified*; PETG + TPU printed solid is the usual
  hobbyist choice. Wash before first use, replace gaskets when they discolour.
